## THE CAUSE

Explain the mechanism, not just the finding. A pull quote is a good place for the one-sentence version:

> The flag was global, the CUDA context was shared, and nobody had connected those two facts.

Lists are fine for parallel points:

- What the code assumed.
- What the runtime actually did.
- Why the two disagreed only under load.

Math is LaTeX between dollar signs. Inline, $t_{\text{total}} = n \cdot t_{\text{launch}} + \sum_i t_i$, or on its own line:

$$
\text{util} = \frac{\sum_i t_i}{t_{\text{total}}} \approx 17.2\%
$$
